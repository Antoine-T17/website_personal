Professional Summary

PhD candidate in aquatic ecotoxicology at the Laboratoire Eau Environnement et Systèmes Urbains (LEESU, Université Paris-Est Créteil). My research focuses on the effects of micro- and nanoplastics and their associated additives on the freshwater crustacean Daphnia magna. I combine standardised and sub-acute bioassays, behavioural endpoints and the chemical characterisation (target and non-target screening) of plastic leachates. I previously worked on optical brighteners, glyphosate and AMPA, using microalgae, freshwater biofilms and zebrafish larvae. I teach biostatistics and ecotoxicology (about 125 hours since 2024), supervise interns and analyse my data in R, with a particular taste for data visualisation and reproducible reporting with Quarto.
